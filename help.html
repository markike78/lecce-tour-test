How to use the Lecce Tour Test app

A phone-friendly, offline version of the VoiceMap test sheet for the tour The southern lady of Baroque. You walk the tour with VoiceMap playing, and record any problems here, one track at a time.

1. Install it on your phone

Do this once, on wifi, before the walk. Address: markike78.github.io/lecce-tour-test

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the address in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (it may say Install app).

Android (Microsoft Edge)

  1. Open the address in Edge.
  2. Tap the ≡ menu at the bottom, then Add to phone.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the address in Safari (not another browser).
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

A Lecce Test icon appears on your home screen. Always open the app from that icon.

2. Check it works offline

  1. Open the app from the icon, tap All tracks, and scroll to the bottom. It should say ✓ Ready to use offline.
  2. Turn on airplane mode.
  3. Enter something on a test track, close the app completely, and reopen it from the icon.
  4. If your entry is still there, you're set. Turn airplane mode off.
  5. Clear the test entry with Erase all entries at the bottom of All tracks (tap it twice).

Also download the tour in the VoiceMap app before you go, so the audio plays without a signal too.

3. On the walk

The app shows one track per screen, numbered like the test sheet (1 to 44). The bar along the top shows how many you've checked.

  • No problems with this track? Tap the green All good · next › button. It's marked as checked and the next track appears.
  • Something wrong? Record it (below), then tap Save · next ›.
  • ‹ Back returns to the previous track. You can change any track at any time.

Everything is saved on the phone as you tap. There's no save button to remember, and no signal is needed.

Entering distances and seconds

Use − and + to change the number in steps of 5, or tap the number to type an exact value. Rough figures are fine: phone GPS is only accurate to about 5 to 10 metres anyway. Distances are in metres unless you switch to feet on the All tracks screen.

Notes by voice

Tap the Notes box, then the microphone on your keyboard, and speak. For dictation without a signal, download your keyboard's offline language pack before the trip.

Add my GPS position

Adds your current coordinates to the note. Useful for showing exactly where a track should have started: stand on that spot and tap it. The phone may ask for location permission the first time.

5. The All tracks screen

Tap All tracks at the top to see every track:

  • ✓ green: checked, all good
  • ! red: issue recorded, with a summary underneath
  • empty circle: not checked yet

Tap any track to jump to it. This screen also has the metres/feet switch and the buttons for sending results.

6. Sending your results

On the All tracks screen:

  • Send results file opens your phone's share options (email, messaging, Drive, OneDrive and so on) with a spreadsheet file (CSV) attached.
  • Save file to phone saves the same file to your Downloads.
  • Copy issues as text copies a plain-text list of just the problems, ready to paste into an email or message.

The file has the same columns as the printed test sheet, plus a TESTED column showing which tracks you actually walked. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

Send yourself the file once or twice during the walk as a backup. Without a signal, an email waits in your outbox until you're back online.

7. Good to know

  • Your entries live only on your phone. If two people test, each sends their own results.
  • Stick to one browser and the home-screen icon. Entries made in a browser tab don't appear in the installed app, and vice versa.
  • Don't clear the browser's data for this site before you've sent your results; that would erase your entries.
  • If the app is updated, the new version arrives the next time you open it with a connection (sometimes one more reopen is needed). Your entries are kept.
  • If you see "Warning: this browser is not saving entries", you're probably in a private/incognito tab. Open the app from the home-screen icon instead.
